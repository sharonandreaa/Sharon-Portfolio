about me.

I'm Sharon Andrea, an 18-year-old from Chennai, currently pursuing Computer Science Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at VIT Vellore.

Although Chennai is home, a significant part of my childhood was spent in Bangalore. Growing up between these two cities has been a part of my story.

I've explored dancing, badminton, keyboard, and singing without settling into just one thing. I enjoy music, small gestures of care, and the little details that make everyday life interesting.

I'm especially curious about UI/UX design and the ways creativity and technology can meet. I'm fascinated by how thoughtful layouts, colours, and small design decisions can completely change the way someone experiences a website or application.

Currently , I'm learning the fundamentals of programming while exploring the possibilities of combining technology with design.
